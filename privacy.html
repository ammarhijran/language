Privacy Policy

Lisan — vocabulary & pronunciation · Last updated 21 November 2026

In short: Lisan has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your learning progress is saved on your device, and Lisan never sends it to the developer or to any server we run. The developer does not collect, receive or sell any of your data.

What Lisan stores

Lisan saves your learning progress (which words you have learned, review dates, daily activity, XP, achievements and goals) and your settings (speech speed, preferred voice, lesson size, daily goal) on your device, in the app’s own storage (on the website: in your browser’s storage for this site). Lisan never sends this data to the developer or to any server we run. You can export it yourself as a file from Settings → Export progress.

Your phone’s backups

If backups are turned on for your phone or tablet, its operating system may include Lisan’s data in them, like that of other apps: Google’s backup on Android, and iCloud (or a backup on your computer) on iPhone and iPad. These backups are made by Google or Apple under your account and their privacy policies; Lisan doesn’t make them, and the developer can’t see them. When you reinstall Lisan or set up a new device from such a backup, your progress may be restored from it. You can manage, delete or turn off backups in your device’s settings.

Deleting your data

  • Settings → Reset progress clears the words and daily history of the language you are learning right now. Your other languages, XP, achievements, goals and settings are kept.
  • Settings → Delete all my data erases everything Lisan has stored on this device: progress in every language, XP, achievements, goals, settings and sync history.
  • Uninstalling the app also deletes its data from the device (on the website: clearing this site’s data in your browser). A copy in your device’s backup, if there is one, stays until that backup is replaced or deleted, and may be restored if you install Lisan again.

Syncing between devices

Lisan has no accounts and no servers. To move progress to another device you create a sync code (or a file containing it) and carry it yourself — by copy and paste, AirDrop, Nearby Share, a message or note to yourself, or your own cloud storage. Lisan never uploads it. If you add a passphrase, the code is encrypted on your device (AES-256-GCM) before you share it, so the apps or services you use to carry it can’t read it. Sync codes contain your learning progress only — never your settings, recordings or anything about you.

Microphone and speech recognition

Lisan only uses the microphone when you tap the microphone or Record myself button to practise pronunciation.

  • Pronunciation scoring uses your device’s built-in speech recognition service — Apple’s on iPhone and iPad, and your Android device’s speech service (usually Google’s) on Android, or your browser’s on the website. That service may process the audio on your device or send it to its provider to turn it into text, under the provider’s own privacy policy (Apple, Google). Lisan receives only the resulting text, uses it to calculate your score, and does not store or send it anywhere.
  • Record myself keeps your recording in memory just long enough for you to play it back. It is never saved to storage or uploaded.

You can turn off microphone or speech recognition access at any time in your device’s settings; the rest of the app keeps working.

Text-to-speech

Words are read aloud by your device’s built-in text-to-speech voices. Some voices (for example Microsoft Edge’s online voices on the website) are provided by the voice’s maker over the internet, under that provider’s privacy policy.

Reminders

In the Android and iOS apps you can switch on a daily study reminder. It is a notification scheduled on your device by the app itself; no server is involved and nothing is sent anywhere. You can turn it off in Lisan’s Settings or in your device’s notification settings.

Children

Lisan does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.
