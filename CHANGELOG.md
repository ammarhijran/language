# Changelog

## Unreleased

### Changed
- **Practice screen:** the ten modes are compact cards (icon and name) with one line below describing the selected mode, so “What to practise” and Start are reached with much less scrolling.

### Added
- **Getting started:** for Ukrainian and Arabic the welcome steps ask “How do you speak?” (as a man / as a woman), so the right first-person forms are used from the first lesson; and the last step offers “Travelling soon? Start with the trip essentials” as an alternative first lesson.
- **Read the script** (Practice, for Japanese, Arabic and Ukrainian): a word is shown in its script with no audio and no reading, and you pick how it is read from four options of similar length — rōmaji, the transliteration, or the “say it like” respelling. For getting off the reading aids.
- **More ear training:** about twice as many minimal pairs for Ukrainian, Japanese and Arabic (20 each), and five more for Turkish (24) — new contrasts such as Ukrainian г / х and hard / soft consonants, Japanese k / g and t / d (てんき / でんき), Arabic د / ض and غ / خ. Every pair is checked to differ in exactly one sound.
- **Numbers by ear** (Practice → Numbers by ear): the voice says a number and you type its digits — four ranges from 0–10 up to 10,000, ten numbers a round. The answer also shows the course’s word for the number where it has one.
- **Four new achievements** (48 in all): Easy Listening (5 hands-free rounds), Sharp Ears (20 sentences built by ear), In My Own Words (practise a text of your own) and Ready to Travel (learn all the trip essentials of a language).
- **Home:** near the start of a language, a “Trip soon? 🎒 Essentials” row leads straight to the trip-essentials crash course.
- **Browser smoke test** (`npm run smoke`, for developers): opens the real app in Chromium for each language and walks about 300 screens — every topic, word pages, the phrasebook, every text, practice modes, My texts, a lesson — failing on any script error, error screen or sideways scroll at phone width.
- **“New in Lisan”** card on Home: shown once to people who already use the app, with links to the features added since their last version (hands-free listening, dictation, My texts, trip essentials, new content, voice and display settings).
- **Handy Extras:** a new phrase topic with 9–12 everyday phrases per language that were missing — “What time is it?”, “I’m looking for a pharmacy”, “Of course”, “Just a moment”, “What is this?”, “I need a taxi”, and in Japanese “Do you speak English?”, “Can you help me?”, “I don’t know”, “No problem”. 1,038–1,171 entries per language in 67 topics now.
- **Trip essentials** (Phrasebook → 🎒 Trip essentials, the first thing a new learner sees there): the 37–41 phrases to learn first for a trip, gathered from all topics in the order you need them, with Practise and Listen hands-free — and **“Learn them step by step”**, which teaches the essentials as short lessons of their own (a crash course before a trip).
- **“Where is the toilet?”** is now a phrase of its own in every language (with its polite or everyday counterpart).
- **“I speak as a woman”** (Settings → Display, Ukrainian and Arabic): first-person phrases that change with the speaker — Я згоден → Я згодна, Я хотів би → Я хотіла б, آسِف → آسِفَة, عَايِز → عَايْزَة — are shown, spoken and checked in the form a woman says (24 Ukrainian and 26 Arabic entries, including their everyday / Egyptian forms). The other form still counts as a right answer, and progress is unaffected.
- **Dictation** (Practice → Dictation): the sentence builder by ear — you hear a sentence (normal or slow, as often as you like) and build it from the word tiles; its meaning appears with the answer. Works with every sentence set the builder has, in all four languages.
- **Compare voices** (Settings → Audio): when the device has more than one voice for the language, each is listed with its quality (Enhanced, Premium, needs internet) — tap ▶ to hear the same sentence in each and keep the most natural one.
- **Two more long speeches per language:** a farewell speech to colleagues, and a short class presentation about a holiday — Ramazan Bayramı, Ukrainian Easter, the Japanese New Year and Eid al-Fitr — each with glossary and questions, and practised like the other speeches (listen & repeat, shadowing, recording). 22 texts per language, 6 of them speeches.
- **More words to tap in every text:** besides a text’s own glossary, any other word or phrase of the course in it can be tapped for its meaning, audio and “Add to reviews” (a quieter underline; whole words only). About three times as many tappable words per text.
- **Word pages:** other words of the course inside the example sentence link to their own pages.
- **Word pages — “In Read & Listen”:** the texts that use the word, each with the line it appears in, one tap from reading it in context (about a fifth of the words appear in a text).
- **My texts** (Read & Listen → My texts): type or paste your own text — a self-introduction, a speech, something your teacher corrected — and practise it like the built-in speeches: the voice reads it line by line (one sentence per line), with listen & repeat scoring, shadowing and recording. Words and phrases of the course that appear in your text are underlined: tap one for its meaning, to hear it, or to add it to your reviews (whole words only — inflected forms are not guessed). Up to 30 texts per language, kept on the device and in backup files (not in sync codes).
- **More vocabulary and phrases (third round):** 8 new word topics (Animals, School & Study, Money & Bank, Eating Out, Sports & Fitness, Arts & Entertainment, Personality & People, More Useful Verbs — 144 words, each with an example sentence) and 6 new phrase situations (At a Restaurant, Renting & Home, Bank, Post & Paperwork, Airport & Border, Problems & Complaints, Feelings & Support — 72 phrases with polite ⇄ everyday pairs) in every language: 1,025–1,158 entries per language in 66 topics now.
- **Hands-free listening** (Practice → Hands-free): a round of up to 20 words or phrases that plays by itself — each one is spoken, you get time to repeat it, you hear it again and then its meaning in English. Pause, skip back and forth, choose how long the pause is; the screen stays on where the browser allows it.
- **Learn:** long topic lists are grouped under “Start here”, “Everyday life” and “Going further”.

- **Delete all my data** in Settings erases every language, XP, achievements, settings and sync history on the device.
- **Android back button** goes to the previous screen (and closes a celebration first) instead of leaving the app.
- **Sync between devices without storing any user data.**
  - **How it works:** a sync code (or file) is created on one device, carried by the learner (copy & paste, share sheet, or their own cloud drive) and merged on the other. There's no account, and Lisan never uploads anything.
  - **Encryption:** with a passphrase, the code is end-to-end encrypted (PBKDF2-SHA-256 210k iterations → AES-GCM-256).
  - **Merging:** a preview shows exactly what will change, and the merge never overwrites anything.
    - Each word keeps its most recent review, and day logs take the larger counts.
    - Achievements, letters and challenge progress are combined.
    - XP and counters are kept per device and added up, so nothing is double-counted.
  - **After a merge:** achievements earned by the combined progress unlock, and the app offers to send a code back for a two-way sync.
  - **Phone apps:** Share and Save as file use the system share sheet (`@capacitor/share`, `@capacitor/filesystem`).
  - **Where to find it:** Settings, and "I already use Lisan on another device" on the welcome screen.
- **Game mechanics, achievements and goals:**
  - **XP for every answer.** Combo bonuses for runs of right answers, plus bonuses for new words, finished and flawless sessions, the daily goal, speaking drills and ear training. Repeating the same word for points is capped at once per word per day.
  - **Levels and titles** (Beginner → Polyglot). The level card appears on the home screen and the Journey page.
  - **34 achievements** across words, mastery, topics, streaks, daily goals, challenges, pronunciation, ear training, the alphabet, combos, flawless sessions, levels and languages. Locked badges show progress, with accessible labels.
  - **Weekly challenges:** 3 per week from a pool of 8, the same for everyone that week, each worth +100 XP.
  - **Personal goals** per language, with an optional target date and a projection based on your last 14 days.
  - **Streak repair:** after missing one day, doing double the daily goal saves the streak.
  - **Celebrations:** confetti overlays that wait until a session ends and group simultaneous rewards on one card. They respect reduced-motion settings.
  - **In sessions:** a live XP chip and combo flame, and a rewards line on the summary screen.
  - **Journey page** (🏆 in the top bar, with a dot for new badges), plus a Settings switch to turn the game layer off.
- **Three new languages: Ukrainian 🇺🇦, Japanese 🇯🇵 and Arabic 🇸🇦**, alongside Turkish. Each has its own vocabulary in the same 12 topics, pronunciation breakdowns, alphabet or kana chart, sound guides and ear-training pairs.
  - **Ukrainian:** 188 words. Stress is marked on every word, since Ukrainian stress is free. Soft consonants, ь and the apostrophe are handled. Answers can be typed in Latin transliteration.
  - **Japanese:** 174 words. A kana reading and Hepburn rōmaji appear under the kanji. Words are split into beats (morae), and long vowels, っ and ん are handled. Answers can be typed in kanji, kana or rōmaji. Speech is matched against both kanji and kana.
  - **Arabic (Modern Standard):** 179 words with full vowel marks and transliteration. Syllables and stress follow standard MSA rules, and the script is shown right to left without flipping the rest of the layout. Answers can be typed with or without vowel marks, or in transliteration. Arabic-Indic digits are understood in speech.

### Fixed
- **Turkish numbers in speaking exercises:** when the phone’s speech recogniser answers with digits (“3” for *üç*, “40” for *kırk*), the answer now counts as right, as it already did in the other languages.
- **Ukrainian stress:** мене / тебе after a preposition are stressed on the first syllable (у ме́не, в те́бе, як на ме́не) — corrected in 12 phrases.
- **Japanese voice:** a number or counter followed by more kanji is read as one word (一日中 “ichinichi-jū”, 一人暮らし “hitori-gurashi”, 七時半), not cut in the middle.
- **Rewards paid twice:**
  - Re-setting an already-reached personal goal no longer pays out again, and neither does syncing a goal completed on another device.
  - Changing the daily goal can't earn it twice in one day, and lowering it still credits today.
  - Ticking whole topics as "already learned" no longer unlocks Topic Master or Completionist.
  - Speaking achievements count different words.
- **Sync:**
  - Unmarked words and removed goals no longer come back after syncing.
  - Practice on two devices on the same day is added up, not undercounted.
  - Reset progress stays on its own device.
  - Damaged or crafted sync codes and backups are cleaned before merging, and can no longer freeze or break the app.
  - Encrypted codes need an 8+ character passphrase and use 600k PBKDF2 iterations.
- **Spaced repetition:** failing a word several times in one session lowers its ease only once, and due dates no longer shift around daylight-saving changes.
- **Audio:** leaving a card stops its recording and speech recognition, so the old word no longer plays over the next screen.
- **Language-specific text:**
  - Praise words and speaking scores are now in each course's own language; before, every course used Turkish.
  - "Letters to work on" shows letters as written: katakana stays katakana, and Arabic keeps its vowel marks.
- **Keyboard and screen readers:** focus moves to each new screen's heading, and the celebration dialog can be used fully from the keyboard.
- **Native apps:**
  - The app reopens in the language you were learning.
  - A failed speech-recognition start no longer cuts off the next attempt, and voices load reliably while the phone's speech engine starts.
  - The iOS privacy manifest declares the file-timestamp reason that App Store Connect requires.
- **Pronunciation data:** Turkish *cumartesi* stress, *şehir* respelling, Arabic stress never on the article, MSA *tadhkira*, Ukrainian в → w, and ear-training pairs that really differ in one sound.
- **Layout:** small-screen overflow and colour contrast fixes.

### Compatibility
- Sync codes and backups from this version are only partly readable by the previous one. Update every device you sync.
- CSS custom properties passed through the DOM helper (`style: { '--pct': … }`) were silently ignored, so the pronunciation score ring never showed its fill.
