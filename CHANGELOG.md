# Changelog

## Unreleased

### Added
- **Only your language is loaded:** the app starts with just the pack being learned (the others load on demand — language switch, a sync that brings their progress). First render on a slow phone CPU 628 → 493 ms, 22 fewer files, about a third less memory; the website also preloads its modules instead of discovering them one level at a time.
- **Daily reminder** in the Android and iOS apps (Settings → Daily reminder): a local notification at the time you choose that says what is waiting, skips today once your goal is met, and stops by itself after two weeks without opening the app. No exact-alarm permission is used; nothing leaves the device.
- **Design pass:**
  - Arabic and Japanese render in proper Naskh / Japanese fonts at readable sizes, Arabic examples line up with their transliteration, and Japanese readings never leave a dangling "·".
  - Separate colours for actions (indigo), progress and success (green), the brand (logo red) and errors, so finished topics no longer look like alerts; better dark-mode surfaces and visible progress tracks.
  - A consistent type, spacing and radius scale, and every control at least 44 px.
  - Polished screens: a short step-by-step onboarding without app chrome; a clearer Progress page (full topic names, chart labels, empty states); a compact Speak lab (kana as a gojūon table, Arabic letters right to left, letter details in a sheet); Journey badges as medals with an "Almost there" strip; grammar lessons with section chips, phone-friendly tables and a one-question-at-a-time quiz.
  - Smaller fixes: back links that follow where you came from, ★ on word pages, a sync summary that counts saved phrases, the Android back button asks before leaving a lesson, and search understands Egyptian spellings (hesab → الحساب).
- **New navigation:** Home (Today) · Learn · Practice · Phrasebook · Me, with a search button in the top bar. Home shows one clear next step ("Review N" / "Continue <topic>" / next topic), shortcuts and the day's word instead of a 28-card grid; Learn is split into Words, Phrases, Talk like a local, Grammar and Read & Listen with compact topic tiles; topic pages list every entry.
- **Phrasebook** with search, situation chips, a polite ⇄ everyday (MSA ⇄ Egyptian) switch, ★ favourites synced between devices, and a big-text Show mode.
- **Global search** for words, phrases, topics, grammar and texts; **continue where you left off**; a learning path that interleaves phrases and everyday speech; leaving a lesson keeps the words you've seen (with a "Leave this lesson?" prompt).
- **Full-screen lessons** with a bottom answer sheet: Continue is visible after every answer on every screen size (was 53% at phone sizes), clear right/wrong colours with the answer, a short shake / pop and a tiny vibration on wrong answers (reduced-motion aware).
- **Read & Listen:** 16 graded texts per language (dialogues — including everyday Egyptian Arabic —, stories, everyday texts and short speeches; A1–B1), each with translations, kana/transliteration, a tap-a-word glossary and comprehension questions. Line-by-line player (Play all, slow, translation and reading toggles, Listen first), speech practice (listen & repeat with scoring, shadowing with fading text, full recording up to 1:30) and dialogue role-play. XP, 5 new achievements, synced.
- **Much more vocabulary and phrases:** 12 new word topics (Months & Dates, Family, Clothes, Hobbies, Countries & Languages, Weather, Groceries, Doctor & Pharmacy, Phone & Internet, Around Town, Kitchen, Jobs — about 235 words, all with example sentences) and 12 new phrase situations (Language Help, Introductions, At Someone's Home, Wishes & Occasions, Doctor, Phone & Messages with real texting forms, Hotel, Emergencies, Transport, Work, Opinions, Friends — 144 phrases with polite ⇄ everyday pairs) in every language: 809–942 entries per language now.
- **Pronunciation that matches what you see:** a speech layer chooses the text and voice per entry — Arabic in pausal form and Egyptian entries with an Egyptian voice, Japanese ambiguous kanji with the right reading, better voice ranking (enhanced/neural first, no novelty voices), never a wrong-language voice, natural slow mode with pauses, syllable chips that say what they show (particle は → wa), and longer listening for speeches.
- **Accuracy:** word-aware speech scoring (a different word or the opposite meaning no longer counts as right); spelling variants, homophones and Egyptian spellings accepted; typed answers that are another word are named as such; common romanisations accepted; the sentence builder accepts valid alternative word orders and no longer gives positions away; multiple choice never offers a second correct answer; reviews credit overdue recall, add slight interval fuzz and don't punish typing slips.
- **Faster and sturdier:** an instant loading screen with a friendly fallback if startup fails; a versioned cache-first service worker (repeat visits on slow networks ~1 s instead of 8–40 s, with an "Update ready" prompt); batched storage writes (1 instead of 78 per review session) that are never lost on close; the word list renders incrementally (~60 ms instead of ~300 ms); a warning when progress can't be saved.
- **Sentence builder:** practise the example sentences instead of only reading them. The English meaning is shown (the sentence can be played, also slowly), and the learner taps shuffled word tiles into the right order; a wrong answer shows the right sentence, its reading and the learner's order.
  - **Practice → Sentences** builds 10 sentences from any topic, the words you've learned, or everything: phrases and their everyday/proper counterparts, example sentences and grammar examples (about 230–390 per language).
  - **Grammar lessons** end with "Practise these sentences" for the lesson's examples.
  - **Reviews** sometimes ask a familiar phrase as a sentence to build.
  - Japanese tiles are the kana reading (the kanji sentence is shown after answering); Arabic tiles read right to left. Punctuation, capitals and Arabic vowel marks don't count, and identical words may swap places. Tiles work with the keyboard (Tab and Enter, or number keys; Backspace takes the last one back).
- **Much more everyday content, chosen by frequency — about 1,000 new entries across the four languages.** Each language goes from 12 to 28 topics, in three groups shown on Learn, Home and Practice:
  - **Words** (+130 per language): More Everyday Verbs, Daily Routine, Feelings & Opinions, Shopping & Money, Connecting Words, Work & Study.
  - **Phrases** (+80): Small Talk, Café & Restaurant, Shopping, Getting Around, Making Plans, Reactions, Problems & Help, Polite Requests.
  - **Talk like a local** (+45): everyday spoken forms and slang.
  - Totals: Turkish 561, Ukrainian 443, Japanese 429, Arabic 434 words and phrases.
  - Every file was written by an author and then checked and corrected by a native-level editor (stress, readings, vowel marks, register labels, naturalness).
- **Polite vs everyday speech.** Entries carry a register (formal · polite · neutral · casual · slang) and, where people say it differently, the counterpart: lessons and word pages show **"What people say"** next to **"Proper / polite"**, both playable. Examples:
  - Turkish *Naber?* ↔ *Nasılsınız?*
  - Ukrainian ти ↔ ви forms
  - Japanese casual ↔ です/ます and keigo
  - Arabic: everyday **Egyptian** next to **Modern Standard Arabic**. The Arabic transliteration gains e, o, ē, ō and g for Egyptian sounds.
- **Grammar lessons:** 12 per language (A1–B1, 48 quiz questions each). Each lesson has short explanations, tables, playable examples with readings, and a 4-question quiz. The last lesson in every language covers how people really speak, e.g. *geliyom / napıyon*, colloquial Ukrainian, Japanese contractions, MSA vs Egyptian.
  - Lessons earn XP and sync between devices.
  - New achievements: First Rules, Grammar Geek, Grammarian, Rule Master and Talk Like a Local.
- **Egyptian Arabic sounds like Cairo:** dialect entries are stressed by Cairene rules (*mush-KI-la*, *maʿ-LĒSH*), and typed answers accept Arabizi numbers (*ma3lesh*, *5alas*, *7abibi*).
- **Independent content audit:** after the native-level edit, separate reviewers checked every new entry and lesson in each language again (form & pronunciation, usage & register, grammar), and every finding was re-checked before it was applied (Turkish 18, Japanese 6, Ukrainian 9, Arabic 15 fixes).
- **Readings for example sentences:** every Japanese example now shows its kana and rōmaji, and every Arabic example its transliteration (178 + 172 sentences, written by one linguist and checked by another). Japanese example sentences can now be practised in Sentences mode too (394 sentences, up from 233).
- **An example sentence for every core word:** 729 new everyday sentences (Turkish 242, Ukrainian 167, Japanese 159 with kana, Arabic 161 with transliteration), each written by one linguist and checked by another. Every word topic can now be practised in Sentences mode: 624 / 529 / 552 / 486 sentences to build.
- **Delete all my data** in Settings erases every language, XP, achievements, settings and sync history on the device.
- **Android back button** goes to the previous screen (and closes a celebration first) instead of leaving the app.
- **Sync between devices without storing any user data.**
  - **How it works:** a sync code (or file) is created on one device, carried by the learner (copy & paste, share sheet, or their own cloud drive) and merged on the other. There's no account, and Lisan never uploads anything.
  - **Encryption:** with a passphrase, the code is end-to-end encrypted (PBKDF2-SHA-256 210k iterations → AES-GCM-256).
  - **Merging:** a preview shows exactly what will change, and the merge never overwrites anything.
    - Each word keeps its most recent review, and day logs take the larger counts.
    - Achievements, letters and challenge progress are combined.
    - XP and counters are kept per device and added up, so nothing is double-counted.
  - **After a merge:** achievements earned by the combined progress unlock, and the app offers to send a code back for a two-way sync.
  - **Phone apps:** Share and Save as file use the system share sheet (`@capacitor/share`, `@capacitor/filesystem`).
  - **Where to find it:** Settings, and "I already use Lisan on another device" on the welcome screen.
- **Game mechanics, achievements and goals:**
  - **XP for every answer.** Combo bonuses for runs of right answers, plus bonuses for new words, finished and flawless sessions, the daily goal, speaking drills and ear training. Repeating the same word for points is capped at once per word per day.
  - **Levels and titles** (Beginner → Polyglot). The level card appears on the home screen and the Journey page.
  - **34 achievements** across words, mastery, topics, streaks, daily goals, challenges, pronunciation, ear training, the alphabet, combos, flawless sessions, levels and languages. Locked badges show progress, with accessible labels.
  - **Weekly challenges:** 3 per week from a pool of 8, the same for everyone that week, each worth +100 XP.
  - **Personal goals** per language, with an optional target date and a projection based on your last 14 days.
  - **Streak repair:** after missing one day, doing double the daily goal saves the streak.
  - **Celebrations:** confetti overlays that wait until a session ends and group simultaneous rewards on one card. They respect reduced-motion settings.
  - **In sessions:** a live XP chip and combo flame, and a rewards line on the summary screen.
  - **Journey page** (🏆 in the top bar, with a dot for new badges), plus a Settings switch to turn the game layer off.
- **Three new languages: Ukrainian 🇺🇦, Japanese 🇯🇵 and Arabic 🇸🇦**, alongside Turkish. Each has its own vocabulary in the same 12 topics, pronunciation breakdowns, alphabet or kana chart, sound guides and ear-training pairs.
  - **Ukrainian:** 188 words. Stress is marked on every word, since Ukrainian stress is free. Soft consonants, ь and the apostrophe are handled. Answers can be typed in Latin transliteration.
  - **Japanese:** 174 words. A kana reading and Hepburn rōmaji appear under the kanji. Words are split into beats (morae), and long vowels, っ and ん are handled. Answers can be typed in kanji, kana or rōmaji. Speech is matched against both kanji and kana.
  - **Arabic (Modern Standard):** 179 words with full vowel marks and transliteration. Syllables and stress follow standard MSA rules, and the script is shown right to left without flipping the rest of the layout. Answers can be typed with or without vowel marks, or in transliteration. Arabic-Indic digits are understood in speech.
- **Language switcher:** use the language pill in the top bar, the language step on the welcome screen, or Settings. Progress, streaks and daily goals are kept separately for each language, and the chosen voice is remembered per language.
- Shared language-pack builder (`makePack`) and tests that check every pack.
- **Already learned**: mark words you already know so lessons skip them.
  - On a new-word card in a lesson, tap **Already learned**; that word's quiz questions are skipped.
  - Tick words in bulk per topic on the *Already learned* screen (Learn page or Progress page). Unticking undoes it.
  - Toggle it on any word's page.
  - Already-learned words come back once for a quick check after about a month. Get one wrong and it returns to normal practice.
  - They have their own colour in the Progress mastery chart, and they don't count toward the daily goal.
- **Welcome screen** on first launch: pick a daily goal (Casual 10 · Regular 20 · Serious 40 · Intense 60 exercises), check that Turkish audio works, and learn how speaking practice uses the microphone.
- **Daily goal** ring on the home screen, a "goal reached" celebration, and a daily-goal setting.

### Fixed
- **Rewards paid twice:**
  - Re-setting an already-reached personal goal no longer pays out again, and neither does syncing a goal completed on another device.
  - Changing the daily goal can't earn it twice in one day, and lowering it still credits today.
  - Ticking whole topics as "already learned" no longer unlocks Topic Master or Completionist.
  - Speaking achievements count different words.
- **Sync:**
  - Unmarked words and removed goals no longer come back after syncing.
  - Practice on two devices on the same day is added up, not undercounted.
  - Reset progress stays on its own device.
  - Damaged or crafted sync codes and backups are cleaned before merging, and can no longer freeze or break the app.
  - Encrypted codes need an 8+ character passphrase and use 600k PBKDF2 iterations.
- **Spaced repetition:** failing a word several times in one session lowers its ease only once, and due dates no longer shift around daylight-saving changes.
- **Audio:** leaving a card stops its recording and speech recognition, so the old word no longer plays over the next screen.
- **Language-specific text:**
  - Praise words and speaking scores are now in each course's own language; before, every course used Turkish.
  - "Letters to work on" shows letters as written: katakana stays katakana, and Arabic keeps its vowel marks.
- **Keyboard and screen readers:** focus moves to each new screen's heading, and the celebration dialog can be used fully from the keyboard.
- **Native apps:**
  - The app reopens in the language you were learning.
  - A failed speech-recognition start no longer cuts off the next attempt, and voices load reliably while the phone's speech engine starts.
  - The iOS privacy manifest declares the file-timestamp reason that App Store Connect requires.
- **Pronunciation data:** Turkish *cumartesi* stress, *şehir* respelling, Arabic stress never on the article, MSA *tadhkira*, Ukrainian в → w, and ear-training pairs that really differ in one sound.
- **Layout:** small-screen overflow and colour contrast fixes.

### Compatibility
- Sync codes and backups from this version are only partly readable by the previous one. Update every device you sync.
- CSS custom properties passed through the DOM helper (`style: { '--pct': … }`) were silently ignored, so the pronunciation score ring never showed its fill.
