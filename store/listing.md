## Full description (App Store & Google Play)
_1585 / 4000 characters_

```
Learn Turkish words — and learn to say them properly.

Lisan teaches the Turkish you actually need, one word at a time, and helps you pronounce every one of them with confidence.

LEARN WORDS THAT STICK
• 306 everyday words and phrases in 12 topics: greetings, numbers, food, family, time, travel, verbs and more
• Short lessons introduce 5 words at a time with audio, example sentences and usage notes
• Spaced repetition brings each word back right before you'd forget it
• Exercises grow with you: listening, reading, multiple choice, typing (with ç ğ ı ö ş ü keys) and speaking

SPEAK LIKE YOU MEAN IT
• Hear every word in a native Turkish voice, at normal or slow speed
• See each word split into syllables, with the stressed syllable highlighted
• IPA and an easy "say it like" guide (merhaba → MEHR-hah-bah)
• Say a word out loud and get an instant pronunciation score — Lisan highlights exactly which letters to work on
• Record yourself and compare your voice with the native audio

MASTER THE TRICKY SOUNDS
• The full Turkish alphabet with sounds and examples
• Guides to ı, ö, ü, ğ, the tapped r, word stress and vowel harmony
• Ear training with minimal pairs like kır / kir, on / ön and cam / çam

TRACK YOUR PROGRESS
• Daily streak, 14-day activity chart and mastery for every topic
• Searchable word list with your learning status
• Practice mode for extra rounds on any topic

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No account. No ads. No tracking. Lisan saves your progress on your device and never sends it to us or to any server we run. You can export it any time.

Merhaba! Let's start.
```

## App Review notes (App Store Connect → App Review Information)
_612 / 4000 characters_

```
No sign-in is required; all features are available immediately.

To try pronunciation scoring: open any word (Progress tab → tap a word) or the Speak tab → Speaking, tap the red microphone, allow microphone and speech recognition access, and say the Turkish word shown. On iPhone, tap the microphone again when finished. Audio uses the device's Turkish voice; if none is installed, iOS downloads Yelda on request (Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content → Voices → Turkish).

All learning content ships inside the app and works offline; speech recognition may need a network connection depending on the device.
```
