## Full description (App Store & Google Play)

```
Learn Turkish, Ukrainian, Japanese or Arabic — the words people really use, and how to say them properly.

Lisan teaches everyday language one small step at a time, and helps you pronounce every word with confidence.

WORDS AND PHRASES THAT STICK
• 800–940 everyday words and phrases per language in 52 topics, chosen by how often people use them
• Short lessons with audio, example sentences and usage notes
• Spaced repetition brings each word back right before you'd forget it
• Exercises grow with you: listening, reading, multiple choice, typing, speaking and building sentences

HOW PEOPLE REALLY TALK
• Polite and everyday forms side by side — what the textbook says, and what people actually say
• Slang and casual speech, clearly labelled
• Arabic: Modern Standard Arabic next to everyday Egyptian
• A phrasebook for 22 situations with favourites and a big-text mode to show your phone to someone

READ, LISTEN AND SPEAK
• Graded dialogues, stories and short speeches with line-by-line audio, translations and tap-a-word meanings
• Practise speeches by repeating, shadowing and recording yourself; role-play dialogues
• Grammar lessons from first sentences to everyday speech, each with a quick quiz

PRONUNCIATION YOU CAN SEE
• Every word split into syllables with the stress marked, IPA and an easy "say it like" guide
• Kana and rōmaji for Japanese; full vowel marks and transliteration for Arabic
• Say a word and get an instant score that shows which letters to work on
• Alphabet and kana charts, sound guides and ear training

STAY ON TRACK
• Daily goal, streaks, weekly challenges and achievements
• An optional daily reminder that tells you what is waiting
• Sync between your devices with a code — no account, no server

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No account. No ads. No tracking. Lisan saves your progress on your device and never sends it to us or to any server we run. You can export it any time.
```

## App Review notes (App Store Connect → App Review Information)
_912 / 4000 characters_

```
No sign-in is required; all features are available immediately. The app teaches four languages; the first screen asks which one (it can be changed later from the language button in the top bar).

To try pronunciation scoring: Practice tab → Speak, or open any word and tap the microphone; allow microphone and speech recognition access, and say the word shown. On iPhone, tap the microphone again when finished. Audio uses the device's text-to-speech voice for the chosen language; if none is installed the app explains how to add one (iOS: Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content → Voices).

The optional daily reminder (Me → Settings → Daily reminder) is a local notification scheduled on the device; notification permission is requested only when the user switches it on.

All learning content ships inside the app and works offline; speech recognition may need a network connection depending on the device.
```
